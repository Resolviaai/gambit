Frequently Asked Questions

What video formats and durations do you handle? +
Vertical short-form edits (15-60 seconds), 4K commercial cuts, documentary storytelling, and motion graphics animations. All exports are delivered in high-bitrate 4K UHD or 1080p ProRes / MP4.
What is your typical turnaround time? +
Short-form cuts and short edits are delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Long-form video projects typically take 3 to 5 business days for the initial assembly. Rush options are available.
What software do you edit with? +
Adobe Premiere Pro CC and DaVinci Resolve Studio for timeline cutting and color grading, After Effects for motion graphics and camera animations, and Audition for audio polishing.
How do revisions work? +
Revisions are organized directly through Discord or Frame.io with timestamped markers. Minor adjustments and pacing tweaks are unlimited until you are fully satisfied.
How do we start a project? +
Email me at antetasks@gmail.com or add me on Discord at gambit.one with your raw footage link, target duration, and references.
